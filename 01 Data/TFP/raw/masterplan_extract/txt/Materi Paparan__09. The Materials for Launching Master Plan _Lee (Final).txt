Transforming Productivity: Strengthening Synergies across Government, Regions, and Business
The Strategic Roadmap: Action Matters
•
Implementation must extend beyond strategy design. Indonesia possesses many 
productivity-oriented policies; however, institutional overlaps, policy 
inconsistencies, and limited cross-agency coordination reduce their impact.
•
Thus, the strategies delineated in the preceding slides to deliver meaningful 
policy outcomes, focused and sustained whole-of-government alignment is 
essential. This master plan’s five-year horizon presents a crucial window of 
opportunity to act, adjust, and embed a productivity-focused development 
trajectory.

11
Transforming Productivity: Strengthening Synergies across Government, Regions, and Business
What Next? Focus on Consistency and Continuity
•
Indonesia is not constrained by a lack of policies but  
challenges in ensuring policy consistency, reducing 
institutional overlap, and overcoming fragmented 
implementation;
•
Gaps in coordination among public institutions further 
compound this complexity.
•
Thus, these systemic issues call for focused and 
continued attention from the GOI to translate strategy 
into sustainable and meaningful impact;
•
It is essential to strengthen institutional coherence by 
clarifying roles and mandates and fostering whole-of-
government alignment to advance the productivity agenda 
in an accountable, adaptive, and future-ready manner.
